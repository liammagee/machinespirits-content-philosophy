2 public artifacts

  • G. W. F. Hegel · 1894 · Dispatch 2026-08-09

    Hegel’s Philosophy of Mind, §§430–436

    Read the movement from recognitive self-consciousness to reciprocity, not only the familiar struggle between master and servant. The practical question is what an encounter permits each participant to become.

  • John Dewey · 1910 · Dispatch 2026-08-09

    How We Think, Chapter I

    Read Dewey’s account of hesitation as an active phase of inquiry. The practical question is how a tutor can delay closure just long enough to ask for evidence that changes which explanation should guide the next move.
