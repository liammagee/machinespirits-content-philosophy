Own and extend your work

All content is saved in your browser's local storage. Export a project file before changing devices or clearing browser data.

The HTML runs offline. Students can modify it with a text editor, Claude Code, or another coding agent. Static hosting requires no server-side code. PDF extraction, AI integration, and shared group syncing are not implemented.
