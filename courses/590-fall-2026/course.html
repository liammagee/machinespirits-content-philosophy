AI and Academic Literature

Welcome to EPOL 590: AI and Academic Literature. This course addresses a fundamental question: how do we conduct scholarly research in an era of generative AI?

Course Overview

This course is both foundational and future-oriented:

  • Foundational: How to develop a rigorous literature review
  • Future-Oriented: How AI is changing scholarship
  • Practical: Each week’s method is applied to your own research project

Seven online seminars meet on Mondays, 5:00–7:50 PM Central. Each staged task is due the Monday after its seminar at 11:59 PM Central, on Canvas.

The Literature Review Recipe

  1. Define your research question
  2. Search and map the field
  3. Read closely and distantly
  4. Identify gaps and contributions
  5. Structure your argument
  6. Write with appropriate framing language

Assessment

Students build one literature review across the course. Each staged task develops a part of it, and the final review brings them together.
