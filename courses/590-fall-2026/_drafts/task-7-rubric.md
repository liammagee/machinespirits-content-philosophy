# Task 7 rubric (draft for review)

Final literature review **or** course outline · 100 points · 40% of the course grade. The six criteria are the syllabus's common criteria; each row says how it reads for Option A (review) and Option B (course outline).

| Criterion | Points | Excellent | Proficient | Developing | Beginning |
|---|---:|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Question, purpose and scope**<br>A: a significant, feasible question with clear boundaries. B: a defined audience, aims and scope for the course. | 15 | 14–15: Clear, significant and well bounded; the reader knows exactly what is covered and why. | 11–13: Clear and feasible; some boundaries or significance left implicit. | 7–10: Present but broad, vague or only loosely connected to the body. | 0–6: Missing or unworkable. |
| **2. Sources: relevance and traceability**<br>A: the literature reviewed. B: the literature that justifies the design, plus the readings assigned. | 15 | 14–15: Well chosen, current where it matters, accurately cited and verifiable. | 11–13: Relevant and traceable, with minor gaps or citation slips. | 7–10: Thin, uneven or partly unverifiable. | 0–6: Unreliable, missing or fabricated sources. |
| **3. Interpretation and synthesis**<br>A: relates sources to one another, explains disagreements, identifies a plausible gap or reframing. B: justifies each design choice from the literature, including where AI belongs and where it does not. | 25 | 23–25: Integrates sources into an argument with a defensible contribution or design rationale. | 18–22: Mostly synthetic, with some source-by-source summary or thin rationale. | 12–17: Largely descriptive; links between sources or choices asserted, not shown. | 0–11: Summary or listing only. |
| **4. Organization, framing and scholarly voice**<br>A: structure, signposting and voice serve the argument. B: the session sequence is coherent, and each session's aims, readings, activities and checks align. | 15 | 14–15: Structure carries the argument; the reader is guided throughout. | 11–13: Clear overall, with some uneven transitions or alignment. | 7–10: Hard to follow in places; structure does not match the argument. | 0–6: Disorganized. |
| **5. Reflexivity and transparent AI use**<br>AI-use statement and positionality, for both options. | 15 | 14–15: Specific about where AI helped, what was checked or rejected, and how the author's position shaped choices. | 11–13: Clear and honest, but general in places. | 7–10: Brief or formulaic. | 0–6: Missing or not credible. |
| **6. Responsiveness to evidence and feedback**<br>Revision memo (250–350 words) and visible development from the staged work. | 15 | 14–15: The memo explains substantive changes and why; the final work shows them. | 11–13: Changes explained but modest, or explained only partly. | 7–10: Little evidence of revision. | 0–6: No memo or no revision. |

Length: 1,500–2,000 words for either option, excluding references, the AI-use statement and the memo. Work well outside the range is marked down under criterion 4.
