First paper: Speed Group Reading, without AI (15 minutes)

Scan and discuss the first paper according to 3 strategies:

•

strategically (what does it really say?) (outside > in)

•

sequentially (how is the argument organized?) (top > bottom)

•

methodologically (what does it do?) (inside > out)

Make notes:

•

•

how is the argument organized (sequential)?

•
