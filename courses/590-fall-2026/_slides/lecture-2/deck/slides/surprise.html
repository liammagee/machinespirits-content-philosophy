Different perspectives, outside the ordinary frame of a discipline or a period. Heteroscopia (“many visions”) is Jaireth’s (2001) term for Indian cinema, and a useful one for thinking about machine vision and generative AI. Less-known or less widely cited authors enrich a review and show awareness beyond the same few authors and studies. Example: histories of AI typically focus on US developments, but Schmidhuber (2022), in his annotated history of modern AI and deep learning, argues for the importance of work in Ukraine (then the USSR), Japan and Finland in the 1960s and 70s.
