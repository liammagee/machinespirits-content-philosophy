Gap Finder · reading the field

A gap is a small centre among large neighbours

Centre: articles that answer your question, combining all your concepts. Its colour is the verdict: red crowded, green gap, yellow too thin

Around it: one sphere per concept, holding the articles that have all your other concepts but not that one

Size is volume: the more articles, the bigger the sphere; every speck stands for the same number of articles

Plenty of research on the parts, little on the combination: that is where a review can add something

Diagram only: the game draws the real counts for your question, live

How to read the game. The centre sphere holds the articles that combine every concept in the question; its colour is the verdict. Each sphere around it holds the articles that combine all the other concepts but not the one on its label. Volume is proportional to the number of articles, and specks have the same density everywhere. A gap shows up as a small centre surrounded by large neighbours: plenty of work on the parts, little on the combination. If a neighbour is tiny too, the question may be too narrow, or that concept goes by another name in this field.
