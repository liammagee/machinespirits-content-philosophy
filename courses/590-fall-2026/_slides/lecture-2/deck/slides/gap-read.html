How to read the game. The inner sphere holds the articles that combine every concept in the question; its outline colour is the verdict. The outer shell holds the articles that combine all but one concept. Every ball is the same size and stands for the same number of articles (the key in the corner says how many), and balls are placed at random, so clusters mean nothing. A gap shows up as a nearly empty centre inside a full shell: plenty of work on the parts, little on the combination. If the shell is nearly empty too, the question may be too narrow, or a concept goes by another name in this field.
