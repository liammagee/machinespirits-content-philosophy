Qualify the gap as the literature grows: add adjectives to narrow it. Then back to step 1

As your literature grows, you may need to qualify the statement of the gap. Maybe there are studies of the general topic X, but you can add adjectives to the gap (your research question) to narrow it down. Go back to step 1, rinse and repeat.
