Mini-game · Gap Finder

Test your question against the literature, one change at a time

Goal

A question that fewer than 25 articles answer, with plenty of research nearby

1 · Start

How is generative AI used in literature reviews? Thousands of articles answer it: crowded

2 · Refine

Add a concept (who, which practice, which issue, where) to narrow it; remove one to broaden it

3 · Check

Click a ball to list a sample of its zone’s articles. Are they really about your question?

4 · Your turn

At a gap, read the draft gap statement. Then Write your own question and try again

Gap Finder, as in lecture-2.md. It makes step 5 of the plan visible: qualify the gap, check, repeat. What students do: start from the crowded default question, add or remove one concept at a time, click balls to check the articles, and when they reach a gap, read the draft gap statement and try their own question. It searches titles and abstracts of journal articles since 2022 in OpenAlex, live. Matching is rough: adding “supervision” also finds banking supervision, which is a good moment to say why every paper has to be checked.
