The review’s own research question

What’s the gap, and how will you show it?

Argue

That the gap exists. It should eventually coincide with your research question.

Search

Choose search terms; AI can translate yours into the ones you don’t know. Read the references of a few good articles.

Organize

By theme: what are the themes? By time: how have debates evolved? Then an introduction and conclusion.

A literature review is a mini research project, so it has its own question: what’s the gap? What you are arguing is that this gap exists (perhaps other things too). Then a methodology: search terms (think about AI’s ability to translate the terms you think of into the ones you don’t), reading the references of a couple of relevant articles, and an organizing principle, by theme or by time. Finally an introduction and a conclusion.
