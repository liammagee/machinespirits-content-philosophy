Designing a literature review

You have a question. What’s next?

One way

Blast out a search

Build a list, annotate, summarize, organize, find the gap. It can come across as arbitrary.

Or

A mini research project

The review has its own question, method and structure, inside the article or dissertation.

Click for each option. The first is how most of us start, and it isn’t wrong, but the result can read as arbitrary. The second treats the literature review as a mini research project within the larger project: it has a question (the gap), a method (how you search and select) and a structure (how you present it). Hour 3 builds that plan.
