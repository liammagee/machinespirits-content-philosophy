More vocabulary: the hermeneutic circle. Understand the whole in order to interpret the path; interpret the path in order to understand the whole.

Look at the whole picture: does it make sense? Fill it in piece by piece. Iterate, whole to part and part to whole. The hermeneutic circle names this: we understand the whole in order to interpret each step, and interpret each step in order to understand the whole.
