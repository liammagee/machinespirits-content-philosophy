Then develop a plan...

1.

Tentative gap - what do you think it is?

2.

Scaffold your review - how many sections? do you need subsections? Add preliminary citations

3.

Develop a sense of key transitions - what's the drama in the review? "also argued", "moreover" etc

4.

Be light, noncommital - prepared to cut/paste

5.

As your literature grows, you might need to qualify the statement of the gap. Maybe there are studies of the general topic X - but you can add adjectives to the gap (research question) to narrow it down... Go back to Step 1, rinse and repeat...
