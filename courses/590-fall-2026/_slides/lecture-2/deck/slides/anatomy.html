One point per click, as in lecture-2.md. The journal article is the vehicle of choice for transmitting science (German Wissenschaft, or what we would call knowledge). We read dozens, hundreds, thousands. Our life is conditioned by the results of journal articles: AI today is dominated by a single paper, Vaswani et al. (2017), “Attention Is All You Need”, with more than 200,000 Google Scholar citations. But what is a journal article? How do you analyze one? How do you write one?
