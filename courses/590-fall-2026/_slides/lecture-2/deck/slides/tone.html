After Snow (1959), The two cultures · next week’s reading

Tone: navigating the two cultures. The scientist, quantitative or positivist tradition: lots of third person and passive voice (“It was observed that…”), though this is changing. The humanist, qualitative, post-positivist, interpretivist or constructivist tradition: more first person and active voice (“We noticed that…”). In education, both co-exist.
