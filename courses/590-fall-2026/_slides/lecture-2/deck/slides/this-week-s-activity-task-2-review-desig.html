This Week's Activity: Task 2 · Review design method and scope (10%)

1.

Write down your assumed audience / field. What do you know about them? Do they prefer lots of recent citations (science)? Or fewer, with more emphasis on the classics (philosophy, theory, some qual studies)?

2.

Create a short plan / recipe method. Include things like:

◦

"Search for these terms / filters"

◦

"Create a database of 10 articles"

◦

"Scan the articles for relevance - discard those that are not relevant"

◦

"With remaining articles: review references, search for more recent citations"

◦

"Once I have 50 articles: code / annototate for relevance"

◦

"Map out review structure; add citations, continue to search under subheadings"

◦

"Supply list of refs to AI and ask if I'm missing anything crucial"

3.

Write down what you imagine or hope will be the gap in the literature (should be close to your research question). Importantly: what would evidence of a gap look like? (this is of course a hard question).

The purpose of the plan / recipe is that it should (ideally) be reproducible by others. Does it pass that test? Next week: share the recipe with others - could they "bake" a literature review to answer the questions
