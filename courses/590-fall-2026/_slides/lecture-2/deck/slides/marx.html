The order of analysis isn’t the order of presentation

In analysis

From the concrete (what we see) to the abstract (concepts)

In presentation

From concepts back to experience, explaining it

Marx distinguished the method of inquiry from the method of presentation. In analysis we move from the concrete, the stuff we see, to the abstract concepts of thought. In presentation we go the other way: from concepts to the world of experience, explaining it.
