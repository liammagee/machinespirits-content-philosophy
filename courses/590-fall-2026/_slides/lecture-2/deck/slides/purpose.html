What is the purpose of an article?

An article makes an argument.

It is persuasive writing: it aims to change a reader’s mind, and it has to use evidence to do it. Every part of an article serves that aim.

The one idea to keep from Hour 1. An article is a form of persuasive writing. It aims to change the mind of a reader, but to do so it must use evidence (and other things). All the parts of an article should contribute to persuasion. Hold onto this when we look at the standard structure.
