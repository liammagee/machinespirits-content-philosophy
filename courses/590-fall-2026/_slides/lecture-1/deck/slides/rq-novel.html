Novelty

Has it been answered before?

  • Often not obvious: many literatures and disciplines work on related problems
  • Is it novel for good reasons, or because no one needs it, or research can’t answer it?
  • Will it stay novel while you work?

“No one has studied the impact of [next year’s model] on student learning” — true until it’s released.

The AI example dates fast, which is the point. A gap defined by a product release closes as soon as everyone studies the release. Look for questions that will still be open when you finish.
