Question and literature

Each one steers the other

Not a question from thin air, nor one that waits for a systematic review.

1 · Draft a topic or question

2 · Search it: is it answered?

3 · Refine what remains, and repeat

Think of it as a dialectic. Draft a question, search on it, and ask whether it’s answered wholly, partly or not at all. What remains becomes the next version of the question. A map can change the question; writing can show the gap was the wrong one. Keep those changes visible in your research record.
