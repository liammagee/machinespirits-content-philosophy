Update Canvas from the revised ERAM 590 syllabus. The working schedule has seven teaching weeks, no break-week submission, six staged tasks worth 10% each, and a 40% final review due Tuesday 15 December 2026 at 11:59 PM Central.

1 Finalize the student syllabus

Open the curriculum and syllabus Draft. Complete the [TO CONFIRM] fields, especially the meeting time, section details, readings, task lengths, rubrics, and policies.

In Canvas, open Syllabus → Edit. Paste the student-facing content from the draft into the editor, then save or update the syllabus. Keep the instructor completion checklist and adaptation notes in this planning workspace. Replace any old syllabus file links so students have one clearly identified current version.

The proposed dates in the text must also be entered into the assignments. Changing prose in Syllabus does not set assignment deadlines.

2 Check the course settings

Open Settings → Course Details. Check the course time zone is the Central Time option for America/Chicago. Confirm the course or term participation settings allow submission through the confirmed 15 December deadline; the last instructional day, 9 December, should not accidentally block the final submission. If institutional settings are locked, ask your Canvas administrator about access.

Do not create a new course shell. Use the existing 590 shell.

3 Bring in the lecture materials if needed

If you have not imported the earlier ERAM590-Canvas-Draft.imscc package, open Settings → Import Course Content, choose Common Cartridge 1.x Package, and upload it. Review the import results and Modules.

That earlier package contains pages and lecture modules, with links to GitHub PowerPoints and externally loaded images. It does not contain the revised syllabus, graded assignments, or the new milestone dates. If it is already imported, edit the existing content rather than importing it again.

4 Set the assignment group weights

In Assignments, create two groups:

Assignment group Group weight Contents
Staged literature review work 60% The six staged tasks, each worth 100 points
Final literature review 40% The final review, worth 100 points

Open the Assignments options menu, choose Assignment Groups Weight, and enable Weight final grade based on assignment groups. Enter 60% and 40%, totaling 100%.

Because the six staged tasks each have the same 100 possible points, each contributes one-sixth of the 60% group, or 10% of the course grade. Keep all six in that group, and do not add drop rules or extra graded items without recalculating. A 100-point final in the other group contributes 40%.

Display grade as Percentage is a display setting; it does not set an assignment’s course weight.

5 Create or edit the seven assignments

For each assignment, open Assignments → + Assignment, or edit the matching assignment if it already exists. Copy its instructions from the syllabus draft, add the final specifications and rubric, set 100 points, select the correct assignment group, and choose the submission type.

Suggested starting submission type: Online → File Uploads for the review documents and map with commentary. If you prefer a shared document, decide the access and submission method before using Website URL or another option.

The final review deadline is confirmed as Tuesday 15 December 2026 at 11:59 PM Central. Other dates remain proposed.

Due identifies the deadline. Available from and Until control access and submission availability. Leave those blank unless you have a reason to restrict access; an Until date at the due time would also prevent late submissions. Check the assigned students and any differentiated dates. Use Save while preparing the course; publish after review.

Rename the imported weekly modules as needed, then add each corresponding assignment to its module using the module’s + control. Add the final-review assignment to the final module even though its confirmed deadline falls after instruction ends.

Replace the 2025 shared Google Doc links, check readings and demonstrations, and review original slides for old dates. The Lecture 1 page and copied 2026 PowerPoint must state the confirmed final deadline: Tuesday 15 December 2026 at 11:59 PM Central.

The faculty grade-reporting deadline is 22 December at 2:00 PM. The confirmed final submission on 15 December leaves about a week for grading.
